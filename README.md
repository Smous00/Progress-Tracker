📊 Project Progress Tracker

The **Project Progress Tracker** is used to monitor and document the progress of the IT Helpdesk System throughout the project lifecycle. It provides a clear overview of completed, ongoing, and upcoming activities while keeping track of individual contributions and project responsibilities.

The tracker records **task progress, team member contributions, stakeholder engagement, challenges encountered, actions taken, and Phase 1 reflections**. It helps the team maintain accountability, identify potential delays, and ensure that project activities remain aligned with the planned objectives and requirements.

The Progress Tracker is regularly updated to provide an accurate record of the project's development and serves as supporting evidence of the group's participation and project management activities.
